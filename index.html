About the creative

Design is the meeting point between story and strategy.

MULTIMEDIA PROFESSIONAL / HARARE

I'm Brandon Mutibvu, a multimedia professional based in Harare, Zimbabwe, working across writing, photography, videography and graphic design.

I hold a BA Honours in Journalism and Media Studies, with a Public Relations major, from the University of Zimbabwe. My work brings visual storytelling, communication and marketing together to make content that looks good and has a job to do.

My current practice spans campaign flyers, client collateral, social media graphics, event materials and product communication. I also work across photography, photo editing and AI-assisted creative workflows.
